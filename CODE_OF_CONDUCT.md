# Code of Conduct

We want bug-solver-pro to be a welcoming, constructive project for everyone. All contributors and participants in project spaces are expected to help maintain an environment where people can collaborate respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful when discussing ideas, feedback, and disagreements.
- Focus criticism on work and reasoning, not on the people doing it.
- Welcome different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Share relevant context, give feedback in good faith, and accept responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect people's privacy and do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized attention without consent, and publishing private information are not acceptable. Demeaning or disruptive conduct that prevents others from participating constructively is also not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing or participating in the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report conduct that violates this code, contact the project owner privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/Rhyru9). Please include what happened, where it happened, and any context needed to understand the report. Keep reports private.

Project maintainers will review reports, protect reporter privacy as far as practical, and take action appropriate to the circumstances. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, or restricting participation in project spaces. Maintainers may decline to act when a report cannot be substantiated.

This code is intended to support a respectful project community; it does not replace applicable platform rules or law.
